Elsa Puspa Silfia

I use machine learning to study complex biological and medical data.

I am pursuing an M.Sc. in Mathematics in Science and Engineering at the Technical University of Munich. My recent master’s thesis at Helmholtz Munich used Xenium 5k spatial transcriptomics data to study cellular niches and treatment-associated changes in gene expression and cell composition.

My interests include single-cell and spatial transcriptomics, perturbation modeling, and multimodal integration to build predictive models of cellular response.
